Gaps

Eight gaps, ranked by impact

1. The menu and footer link to pages that do not exist

High impact · easy fix

Ten internal URLs return a 404 error, and most are linked from every page. That is roughly 340 broken links across the site. Visitors hit dead ends, and Google wastes its crawl on them.

  • /solutions/cyber-security-solutions/ · 78 links
  • /solutions/video-conference-meeting-room-solutions/ · 78 links
  • /chip-level-servicing/ · 53 links
  • /privacy-policy/, /terms-and-conditions/, /careers/, /solutions/, /softwares/ · 26 links each
  • /contact-us/, /queue-management/ · 1 link each

Fix: point each menu and footer link at the live page, and publish the missing Privacy Policy and Terms pages.

2. Navigation still uses old addresses

Medium impact · easy fix

Thirteen old URLs, such as /solutions/cloud-solutions/ and /server-management/, redirect to the new "in-dubai" pages. The menu links to the old versions about 650 times, so every click takes an extra hop and passes less ranking value.

Fix: update the menu, footer and in-page links to the final URLs.

3. Every page has two meta descriptions

Medium impact · easy fix

All 26 pages carry a written description plus a second, auto-generated one that dumps up to 4,000 characters of page text. Google may show either, so the search snippet is unpredictable.

Fix: remove the duplicate tag (usually two SEO plugins or a theme setting) and keep one description of 150 to 155 characters per page.

4. Images are far too heavy

High impact · moderate fix

Twenty-five images are over 100 KB and several are over 1.5 MB. These alone explain most of the 7.5-second mobile load.

  • Network-Cables.png · 2.7 MB
  • ChatGPT-Image-Jul-4-2026.png · 2.4 MB
  • Data-recover.png · 2.1 MB
  • It-repairing-chip.png · 1.9 MB

Fix: convert to WebP, resize to display size, aim for under 150 KB each, lazy-load below the fold, and set width and height on every image.

5. Service pages are thin and hard to read

High impact · ongoing

Service pages run from 364 to 860 words, and all 26 pages score as "hard" or "very hard" to read. Sentences average about five words, which signals fragmented labels and bullet stubs, not explanations. Competing Dubai IT firms answer buyer questions in depth. Two pages (Data Recovery and Book a Consultation) have no main heading.

Fix: rebuild each priority page to 900 to 1,200 words covering scope, process, brands supported, pricing factors, areas served and FAQs.

6. Almost no authority from other websites

High impact · ongoing

The site has 81 backlinks and ranks for one keyword. For competitive terms such as "IT solutions in Dubai", Google needs outside proof that the business is real and trusted.

Fix: UAE business directories, vendor partner listings, supplier and client mentions, and a steady flow of Google reviews.

7. Trust signals are missing or unproven

Medium impact · easy fix

Several page titles claim "No.1 Trusted" or "No.1 Reliable" with nothing to back it up, and four of those titles are too long to display fully. The privacy policy and terms pages are missing, and the careers "Apply Now" form returns an access error.

Fix: replace "No.1" with provable claims (years in business, certified engineers, brands, response time), and add client logos, reviews and case studies.

8. An IT security company without basic security headers

Low SEO impact · credibility risk

295 URLs are missing standard security headers (HSTS, X-Frame-Options, X-Content-Type-Options, Referrer-Policy). Search rankings are barely affected, but a technical buyer who checks will notice.

Fix: add the four headers at server level. It is a one-time change.

Suggestions

A 90-day plan

Repairs first, then content, then authority. Each phase makes the next one more effective.

Days 1 to 30

Repair the foundation

  • Fix the 10 broken URLs and 13 redirected links in the menu and footer
  • Remove the duplicate meta description on all pages
  • Compress and resize the 25 heavy images; set image dimensions
  • Defer render-blocking scripts; enable caching
  • Add missing H1s, privacy policy and terms pages
  • Set up Search Console, Analytics and call and WhatsApp click tracking
Days 31 to 60

Deepen the content

  • Rewrite the top 8 service pages around the keyword map below
  • Rewrite titles without "No.1"; keep them under 60 characters
  • Add FAQ and LocalBusiness structured data
  • Claim or complete the Google Business Profile with services, photos and reviews
  • Publish the first 4 buyer guides
Days 61 to 90

Build authority and demand

  • List on UAE directories and vendor partner pages
  • Publish 3 client case studies
  • Launch area pages for the main business districts
  • Start a focused Google Ads campaign on the highest-intent services
  • Review rankings and leads; set the next quarter's targets
Opportunities

Where the growth is

Recurring-revenue services first

IT AMC, managed IT and IT outsourcing bring contract income, and buyers search for them with clear intent. These pages deserve the deepest content and the first ad budget.

The homepage says UAE, the pages say Dubai

The headline promises "businesses across the UAE", but every service page targets Dubai only. Pages for Abu Dhabi and Sharjah would open two more markets with little extra effort.

Business-district pages

Office IT is bought locally. Pages for Business Bay, DIFC, JLT, Deira, Al Quoz and Jebel Ali free zone match how companies search for a nearby provider.

Google Business Profile and reviews

"Near me" and map results drive most local service calls. A complete profile with regular reviews is the fastest route to visibility while organic rankings build.

Buyer guides that answer cost questions

The site has no blog or resource section. Guides on AMC pricing, CCTV installation cost and new-office IT checklists capture buyers early and earn links.

Product pages by brand

The products page lists Apple, laptops and projectors on a single page. Separate brand and category pages can rank for supplier searches and support bulk enquiries.

Proof: case studies and partner badges

B2B buyers want evidence. Project write-ups with numbers, client logos and vendor certifications turn visitors into enquiries.

Answers for AI search

Clear question-and-answer sections and structured data help the site get quoted in Google's AI answers and in assistants, where competitors are still weak.
